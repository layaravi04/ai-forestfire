# System Limitations & Future Scope

## Technical Limitations
1. **Visual 2D Proxy vs. Ground Surface Area**: RGB bounding box pixel area is a visual 2D perspective projection and does not equal physical ground surface area (hectares/acres).
2. **Nighttime Optical Cameras**: RGB cameras require thermal/infrared sensors for optimal nighttime fire detection.
3. **NASA Satellite Latency**: Satellite pass frequency (MODIS/VIIRS) occurs at discrete orbital intervals (~12-hour revisit time).
