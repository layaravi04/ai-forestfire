# Evaluation Metrics & Benchmarking

## Metrics Evaluated
- **Accuracy**: Ratio of correct detections.
- **Precision & Recall**: Tradeoff between false positives and missed detections.
- **F1 Score**: Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall.
- **mAP@50 / mAP@50:95**: Mean Average Precision for object bounding box localization.
- **Inference Time & Parameters**: Measure of real-time laptop execution efficiency.
