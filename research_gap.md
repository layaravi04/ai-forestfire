# Positioning & Research Gap

## Literature Context
Existing wildfire systems typically isolate either:
1. Single-image classification using 2D CNNs,
2. Real-time object detection via YOLO, or
3. Standalone satellite hotspot tracking.

Our proposed system bridges this gap by unifying ground camera streams, NASA satellite telemetry, spatial-temporal sequence modeling across 5 hybrid architectures, multi-model risk fusion, severity calculation, and interactive GIS mapping.
